Privacy Policy — StepCoin

Last updated: 12 October 2026

StepCoin (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by Khan Force Development. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We have tried to keep the app as private as possible: StepCoin has no login and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

1. Information the app handles

  • Step & activity data. StepCoin reads your step count from your device’s built-in step-counter sensor (using the Physical activity permission) to show your daily steps, distance, calories, and to award in-app coins. This data is processed on your device and stored only on your device. We do not upload it to any server.
  • In-app coins and settings. Your coins, streaks, and Pro status are saved locally on your device. They are not linked to your identity and are not sent to us.
  • We do not collect personal information. StepCoin itself does not create an account for you and does not transmit personal data to Khan Force Development.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of StepCoin shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain data, such as a device advertising identifier and general usage/diagnostic information, in line with Google’s policies. This data is handled by Google, not by us.

You can learn how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. On most devices you can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you purchase StepCoin Pro, banner ads are removed.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

StepCoin Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Your payment is processed by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google records that a purchase was made so your Pro status can be restored on your devices.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Physical activity (Activity Recognition) — to count your steps.
  • Internet — to load ads and process purchases.
  • Vibrate — for the workout timer alert.

5. Children

StepCoin is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention & deletion

Because your step history, coins, and settings are stored only on your device, you can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will reflect any changes.
